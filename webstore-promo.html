NotebookLM Mindmap Exporter

Export mindmaps from NotebookLM as Markdown, FreePlane, or standalone SVG.

  • One-click export to Markdown for easy editing and sharing
  • Export to FreePlane .mm
  • Export to standalone SVG
  • Choose how many levels are exported as Markdown headings before switching to bullet lists
  • Modern, user-friendly popup UI
  • File names include root node and timestamp for easy management

Note: This extension is only intended for use with mindmaps on NotebookLM. To export, simply open a mindmap in a notebook, click the extension, and choose your export format.

Important: The extension exports the current NotebookLM mindmap as Markdown, FreePlane .mm, or standalone SVG using NotebookLM's current page data.

How to use:

  1. Open a notebook page with a mindmap and open the mindmap.
  2. Click the Mindmap Exporter extension icon.
  3. Choose your export format in the popup.
